FAQ

Air-quality or forecast values differ from other sources

Air-quality and water-level data come from Thai public agencies through our own service. They can be delayed or inaccurate, and each station is at a different location from you. Do not rely on the app as your only source for safety decisions. The app is made by an independent developer and is not an app of any government agency.

The app does not show weather around me

Check that location access is allowed in Settings > Privacy & Security > Location Services, or search for a place inside the app. Outside Thailand, station data may not be available.

Premium subscription and cancellation

Premium is an auto-renewing subscription charged to your Apple Account. Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period in Settings > your name > Subscriptions. Refund requests are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

I purchased Premium but it is not active

Restart the app and make sure you are signed in with the same Apple Account used for the purchase. If it still does not work, email us with the user ID shown on the app's settings screen (if available) and the purchase date.
