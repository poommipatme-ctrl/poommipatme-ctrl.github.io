FAQ

What is real and what is simulated?

The µT reading on the Scan tab, the graph, the level lights and the low-frequency sound meter are real values from your phone's sensors. Spirit Box, EVP, the effect camera and spooky events are simulated effects, marked SIM, and the Event Log shows whether each event came from a real magnetic change or from chance. For real values only, choose Science Mode, which turns off all randomness and effects.

The µT value barely moves, or jumps by itself

Move a fridge magnet or a speaker slowly towards the phone. Where the sensor sits differs by model, usually near the top, and the value falls quickly with distance. If it jumps by itself, keep the phone still and away from metal, magnetic cases and wireless chargers.

Why can't it measure 50 Hz mains or find wires in walls?

A phone's magnetic sensor reads too slowly for a field that flips 50 times a second, so it cannot measure it the way a professional meter does. Do not use this app for safety or health decisions, or to find wiring in walls. Lesson 3 in the app explains why.

Does the Spirit Box answer my questions?

No. It plays words picked at random from a fixed word list. It does not listen to you and does not follow any sensor.

I can't hear the effects

On iPhone, turn off the silent switch on the side and turn the volume up; the app always respects the silent switch. On Android, turn up the media volume. Also check that Effect sounds is on in the app's Settings tab.

The microphone or camera does not work

If you declined the permission, open your phone's Settings > Ghost Lab and allow the microphone or camera. The effect camera screen has a button that takes you there.

Turning off flashes, glitches or shaking

In the Settings tab, turn off "Flashes and screen flicker" or "Glitch and shaking images". Full-screen flashes are always limited to 3 per second, and glitches turn off by themselves when Reduce Motion is on in your phone settings.

Where are my saved EVP recordings?

In the app's storage on your phone only; they are never uploaded. This version has no in-app delete button; deleting the app removes them all.
