FAQ

What is real and what is simulated?

The µT reading on the Scan tab, the graph, the level lights, the low-frequency sound meter, the green skeleton of the pose camera and the yellow box of the motion camera are real. Spirit Box, EVP, the images drawn over the camera (including the blurred figure) and spooky events are simulated effects, marked SIM, and the Event Log shows whether each event came from a real magnetic change or from chance. For real values only, choose Science Mode, which turns off all randomness and effects.

The µT value barely moves, or jumps by itself

Move a fridge magnet or a speaker slowly towards the phone. Where the sensor sits differs by model, usually near the top, and the value falls quickly with distance. If it jumps by itself, keep the phone still and away from metal, magnetic cases and wireless chargers.

Why can't it measure 50 Hz mains or find wires in walls?

A phone's magnetic sensor reads too slowly for a field that flips 50 times a second, so it cannot measure it the way a professional meter does. Do not use this app for safety or health decisions, or to find wiring in walls. Lesson 3 in the app explains why.

Does the Spirit Box answer my questions?

No. It plays words picked at random from a fixed word list. It does not listen to you and does not follow any sensor.

What is the green skeleton on the camera?

It is the pose of a real person in the picture, found by AI on your phone, always labelled "Real human pose · on-device AI". The AI can be wrong: it sometimes sees objects such as a chair or a pile of clothes as a person. That is an AI error, not something unseen. The simulated figure of the effects (SIM) is a blur, never lines and dots, and it never follows the AI.

The yellow motion box appears by itself

The box only shows which part of the picture changed. Flickering light, shadows, insects, pets or a moving camera can all cause it. Rest the phone on a stand or a shelf; if the phone moves, the app pauses and shows "Keep the phone still". Sensitivity (low, medium, high), sound and vibration are in the Settings tab.

Ads and Remove ads

A small banner appears only on the Camera & FX and Settings tabs in Fun Mode. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase in the Settings tab removes it for good. After reinstalling or on a new phone, tap "Restore purchases" in the Settings tab with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with.

I can't hear the effects

On iPhone, turn off the silent switch on the side and turn the volume up; the app always respects the silent switch. On Android, turn up the media volume. Also check that Effect sounds is on in the app's Settings tab.

The microphone or camera does not work

If you declined the permission, open your phone's Settings > Ghost Lab and allow the microphone or camera. The camera screen has a button that takes you there.

Turning off flashes, glitches or shaking

In the Settings tab, turn off "Flashes and screen flicker" or "Glitch and shaking images". Full-screen flashes are always limited to 3 per second, and glitches turn off by themselves when Reduce Motion is on in your phone settings.

Where are my saved EVP recordings?

In the app's storage on your phone only; they are never uploaded. This version has no in-app delete button; deleting the app removes them all.

Privacy

Sound and camera images are never recorded or sent off your phone, and the developer has no server. Google AdMob (ads) and, on Android, the pose AI (ML Kit) send device and usage data to Google. Details are in the Privacy Policy.
