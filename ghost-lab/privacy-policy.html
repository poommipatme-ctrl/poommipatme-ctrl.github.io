  • ไมโครโฟน: แอปขอสิทธิ์ครั้งแรกเมื่อคุณเปิด EVP, มิเตอร์เสียงความถี่ต่ำ หรือสวิตช์ไมค์ในหน้าสแกน เสียงถูกประมวลผลในหน่วยความจำของเครื่องขณะใช้งานเท่านั้น และไม่ถูกบันทึก ยกเว้นเมื่อคุณกดปุ่มบันทึกในหน้า EVP ซึ่งจะบันทึกเป็นไฟล์เสียง (.wav) ในพื้นที่เก็บข้อมูลของแอปบนเครื่องของคุณ
  • กล้อง: แอปขอสิทธิ์ครั้งแรกเมื่อคุณเปิดหน้ากล้อง ภาพสดใช้เป็นพื้นหลังของเอฟเฟกต์จำลอง และประมวลผลบนเครื่องเพื่อหาท่าทางของคน (กล้องท่าทาง) และส่วนของภาพที่เปลี่ยน (กล้องจับการเคลื่อนไหว) แอปไม่ถ่ายภาพ ไม่บันทึกวิดีโอ ไม่เก็บภาพไว้ และไม่ส่งภาพออกจากเครื่อง ผลที่ได้มีแค่จุดท่าทางและกรอบบนจอขณะนั้น
  • เซ็นเซอร์การเคลื่อนไหวของเครื่อง (accelerometer และ gyroscope): ใช้ขณะเปิดกล้องจับการเคลื่อนไหว เพื่อรู้ว่าเครื่องวางนิ่งอยู่หรือไม่ ไม่ถูกบันทึกและไม่ถูกส่งออก
  • การตั้งค่า: เช่น โหมด ภาษา เสียง การสั่น ความไวของกล้องจับการเคลื่อนไหว และสถานะว่าซื้อ "ลบโฆษณา" แล้ว บันทึกไว้ในเครื่องเพื่อให้แอปจำค่าที่คุณเลือก
  • ตำแหน่ง: แอปไม่ขอสิทธิ์ตำแหน่งและไม่ใช้ GPS แต่ Google AdMob อาจประมาณตำแหน่งคร่าวๆ จาก IP address (ข้อ 3)

Privacy Policy

Ghost Lab · Effective from Ghost Lab version 1.1.0 · Ads and in-app purchases start with version 1.1.0; earlier versions have neither and send no data off the device.

Summary: there is no account and the developer has no server. Sound and camera images are processed on your device only; they are never recorded or sent. From version 1.1.0 the app shows ads from Google AdMob, which sends device and ad data to Google, and on Android the pose AI (Google ML Kit) sends its own diagnostic data to Google, never images. This data goes to Google, not to the developer.

1. Who is responsible

The app is developed by Poommipat Limsirivong ("the developer"). Contact: poommipat.l@gmail.com

2. What the app uses on your device

  • Magnetometer: used to show the magnetic field reading, the graph and the lessons while that screen is open. The readings are not stored and never leave your device.
  • Microphone: asked for the first time you open EVP, the low-frequency sound meter or the mic switch on the Scan tab. Sound is processed in your device's memory while in use and is not recorded, except when you tap Save on the EVP screen, which saves an audio file (.wav) in the app's storage on your device.
  • Camera: asked for the first time you open the camera screen. The live view is shown behind simulated effects and processed on your device to find the pose of people (pose camera) and the parts of the picture that changed (motion camera). No photo or video is taken, stored or sent anywhere; the only results are the pose points and the box on screen at that moment.
  • Motion sensors (accelerometer and gyroscope): used while the motion camera is on, to know whether the phone is resting still. Not stored, never sent.
  • Settings such as mode, language, sound, vibration, motion camera sensitivity and whether you bought "Remove ads" are saved on your device so the app remembers your choices.
  • Location: the app does not ask for location permission or use GPS, but Google AdMob may estimate a general location from your IP address (section 3).

3. Advertising (Google AdMob, from version 1.1.0)

The banner appears only on the Camera & FX and Settings tabs in Fun Mode, never on the camera, the Scan tab, Science Mode or the lessons, and ad content is limited to PG.

  • Data the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and shares automatically (as Google documents it): IP address (which may be used to estimate a general location), product interactions such as app launches and ad taps, diagnostic information about app and SDK performance, and device identifiers such as the Android advertising ID or, on iPhone if you allow tracking, the IDFA. Used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, encrypted in transit.
  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example the EEA and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before any ad loads.
  • Changing or withdrawing consent: in those regions the Settings tab has an "Ad privacy" section. Tap "Privacy options" to open Google's form again and change or withdraw your consent at any time. If you withdraw it, the app stops requesting new ads at once.
  • iPhone: after the consent form, iOS asks whether the app may track you. If you say no, ads still appear but are not personalized, and the app works the same. You can change this at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Google uses this data under its own policy: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

4. Pose AI on Android (Google ML Kit)

On Android the pose camera uses Google ML Kit with the model inside the app. Images are never sent, but according to Google's documentation ML Kit sends its own diagnostics and usage statistics to Google: device information (manufacturer, model, OS version), the app's package name and version, per-installation identifiers (not meant to identify you or the device), performance metrics such as latency, API configuration, event types and error codes, and for the pose feature Firebase Remote Config and Firebase Installations. It is encrypted in transit, and Google states it is not transferred to third parties. On iPhone the pose camera uses Apple Vision on the device and sends nothing. The motion camera is the app's own code on both systems and sends nothing.

5. In-app purchase ("Remove ads", from version 1.1.0)

Purchases and restores go through the App Store or Google Play. The developer receives no payment details. The app keeps only a "bought" flag on your device and checks it with the store when online. Once bought, the app no longer loads Google AdMob and asks for no consent or tracking permission.

6. Other providers

The developer has no server and receives no data from the app. The app has no analytics or crash reporting of the developer's own. Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) may collect download, purchase and usage information under their own policies.

7. Device backups

If you turn on device backups (such as iCloud or Google backup), your device may include the app's settings and any EVP audio you saved in your own backup. The developer does not receive and cannot access that backup.

8. Deleting data and withdrawing permission

  • Delete the app to remove its settings and all saved EVP audio (this version has no in-app button to delete EVP files). A "Remove ads" purchase stays with your store account and can be restored.
  • You can withdraw microphone, camera or tracking permission at any time in your device settings. The magnetic sensor features keep working.
  • Data Google collects through AdMob or ML Kit is held by Google; the developer cannot access or delete it. Contact Google under the policy in section 3.

9. Children

The app is not designed for children under 13. The developer collects no personal data from anyone, and ad content is limited to PG.

10. If you contact the developer

If you send an email, the developer uses your address and message only to reply to you, never for marketing.

11. Your rights

The developer holds no personal data about you to access, correct or delete; ad and diagnostic data is held by Google. For questions under Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) or the GDPR, use the email above.

12. Changes

If the app changes how it uses data, the developer will update this page before releasing that version.
